# User Guide
Welcome to **BrainBytes**, your AI-powered learning companion. This guide explains how to navigate the platform, manage your learning materials, and maximize the AI's capabilities.

</br>

## Navigation
The application uses a Sidebar for navigation, containing four main sections:

- AI Chat Client
- Learning Dashboard
- Learning Materials
- My Profile

At the bottom of the sidebar, you can **Log In** or **Log Out** at any time.

</br>

## Features
Below are the main features of the BrainBytes Platform. You can see the **video guide** here: [Go to Recording of Features](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1zt61lzrjVZkezEFQP70KPaLSYESRHQf-?usp=sharing)


### 1. AI Chat Client
Interact with the BrainBytes AI Tutor to get instant answers, explanations, and examples.

#### How to Use:
1. **Select a Subject**: Choose a topic (e.g., General, History) from the dropdown menu near the chatbox. This provides context for the AI.
2. **Ask a Question:** Type your query in the input box and press Send.
3. **Receive Response:** The AI will generate a response tailored to your selected subject.

#### User Modes:
|Mode |Features | Data Privacy| 
|---|-----|--------|
|**Guest User** | Instant access without login. No account required.|🔒 **Private & Temporary:** Chats are not saved on the server. History is lost when you refresh or log out. Ideal for quick, private queries.|
**Authenticated User**| All chats are saved. Past conversations are fed back to the AI for better context and continuity. Your preferred subject (set in Profile) loads automatically in the dropdown. | 🧠 **Personalized:** Your data is secure and linked to your account. You can revisit past chat messages anytime.

</br>

### 2. Learning Dashboard
>Note: Available to Authenticated Users Only. Login to access.


Get a snapshot of your learning progress and recent activity.

1. **Recent Activity Logs:** View a list of your most recent questions and AI responses.
2. **Activity Summary:** See the Total Q&A Sets Saved, giving you a quick overview of your engagement.

</br>

### 3. Learning Materials
>Note: Available to Authenticated Users Only. Login to access.

Create, store, and manage your own study notes and curriculum resources directly in the browser.

#### How to Create a Material:

1.**Select Subject:** Choose the relevant subject area from the dropdown.

2. **Enter Details:**
    1. Title: Give your document a clear title.
    2. Content: Paste or type your study notes or summaries.
3. **Commit:** Click `Commit Entry` to save.

#### Managing Materials:
1. **View:** Scroll down to see all your saved materials in a list.
2. **Modify:** Click the `Modify` button on any item to edit the title or content.
3. **Delete:** Click `Delete` to permanently remove a resource. Confirm the action when prompted.


</br>

### 4. My Profile
>Note: Available to Authenticated Users Only. Login to access.

Manage your account settings and preferences.

- **View Details:** See your current Name, Email, and Preferred Subject.
- **Edit Profile:**
    - You can update your Username or Email.
    - You can change your Preferred Subject (this affects the default selection in the AI Chat).
    - Click `Save` Profile to apply changes.

</br>

## Quick Tips
- **Context Matters:** Always select a **Subject** before asking a question. The AI performs significantly better with specific context.
- **Ask Smartly:** The AI automatically detects your **intent** based on keywords. Try starting your questions with:
    - "**What is**..." or "**Define**..." for clear **definitions**.
    - "**How does**..." or "**Explain**..." for detailed **explanations**.
    - "**Give me an example**..." or "**Show me**..." for practical **examples**.
- **Save Your Work:** If you are a **Guest** user, copy important AI responses to a safe area before refreshing or exiting the browser, as they will be lost.
- **Organize:** Use the **Learning Materials** section to build a personal library of notes that you can reference anytime.
